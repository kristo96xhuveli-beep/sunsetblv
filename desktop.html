Our Story

Some stories begin with a grand plan. Ours began with an open door. Back in the early 1990s, our family welcomed travellers into a spare room. By 2000, two purpose-built guest rooms marked a new chapter. In 2004, the next generation opened the bar, bringing music, laughter and a new meeting place to the village. More rooms followed over the years. Today, we continue that family journey with accommodation, drinks and food. Welcome to our home.
